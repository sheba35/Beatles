The Beatles

The Beatles were an English rock band formed in Liverpool in 1960. The group was made up of John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, and Ringo Starr. Together, they became one of the most successful and influential bands in the history of popular music.

During the 1960s, The Beatles transformed popular music with their distinctive sound, creative songwriting, and changing musical style. Their popularity quickly spread from England to the United States and eventually around the world.

Music & Media

The Beatles released a remarkable series of albums throughout the 1960s, constantly experimenting with new sounds and ideas. Songs such as "She Loves You," "I Want to Hold Your Hand," "Yesterday," and "Hey Jude" became some of the most recognizable songs in popular music. The Beatles left not only a lasting mark on music but films as well in:

  • A Hard Day's Night (1964)
  • Help! (1965)
  • Magical Mystery Tour (1967)
  • Yellow Submarine (1968)
